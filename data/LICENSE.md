# Catalog data attribution

Homebrew Cask names, homepage URLs, and short descriptions come from the Homebrew contributors’ BSD 2-Clause licensed recipes. Their copyright notice and full license are distributed at `public/licenses/homebrew.txt`. Record provenance names this source and links to its individual recipe page. Descriptions are prefixed with the product name and may have punctuation/formatting normalized.

Awesome-Selfhosted descriptions are adapted from the community’s CC BY-SA 3.0 licensed list. These adapted descriptions remain under CC BY-SA 3.0; the full license is distributed at `public/licenses/awesome-selfhosted.txt`. Attribution and original-source links are included in each record’s `sources`. Changes remove directory markup, license/language suffixes, and auxiliary links, and prepend the product name. Inclusion in this mixed catalog does not change the licenses of the independent records from other sources or the application source code.

Official website titles are retained as short identity evidence. Capability evidence, where present, is attributed to its actual official URL. An identity check is not a hands-on review or certification of the product's features, security, availability, or fitness.
